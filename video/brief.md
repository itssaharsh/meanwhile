# Meanwhile — demo brief

**One-liner.** Meanwhile turns the world's public webcams into a live channel that an AI
director runs, and it refuses to call a frame live unless it can prove when it was taken.

**Audience.** Hackathon judges. One watch, sound on, possibly at 1.25×. They have seen thirty
demos today and most of them opened on a logo.

**The aha.** The channel cuts to a new city by itself — the chyron turns over, the globe flies,
the score changes — and the new frame carries a teal chip reading `VERIFIED LIVE · 12 MIN OLD`.
The claim and the evidence arrive together. The second aha, ten seconds later: you take the
chair and put a different camera on air yourself, and the bar admits you are directing.

**Problem hook.** Most "live" webcams are lying, and the lie is invisible. A CDN restamps
`Last-Modified` on every request, so a frame shot three hours ago swears it is two minutes old.
That is not a hypothetical — it is why this product cross-checks a frame's claimed age against
the sun at that longitude and throws the timestamp away when they disagree.

**Scope cut — not in the video.** Bring-your-own-key, the email subscribe sheet, the four-rung
country-fetch ladder, `/_kit`, the 404, the provider failover chain. They are all real and all
documented; none of them survive a 150-second cut.
